# Numerical errors

[All issues](../README.md)

These bugs make SWMM compute wrong numbers: a wrong formula or constant, a unit conversion done the wrong way, a result that depends on the time step, or water and pollutant mass booked twice or not at all. Most give no warning, and often the only sign is the continuity error in the report. They are in flow routing (dynamic-wave nodes and pumps, storage units, weirs, culverts, cross-sections, transects and inlets), in runoff (subareas, infiltration, groundwater, LID units, snow melt, evaporation, rainfall and RDII), in water quality routing and treatment, and in control rules. NUM-01, NUM-02 and NUM-45 are 5.3.0 regressions, NUM-42 and NUM-50 are in features added in 5.3.0, and NUM-54 to NUM-59 are in 5.2.4 only; 6.0.0 reproduces 41 of the 57, 11 of them on purpose for parity with the legacy engine.
