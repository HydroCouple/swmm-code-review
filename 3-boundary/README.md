# Boundary conditions

[All issues](../README.md)

These issues are at the edges of a model: outfalls, inlets, ponding junctions and zero-area subcatchments; the start and end of the run, a hot start and a late report start; and valid but extreme inputs such as a culvert on an adverse slope, a negative evaporation adjustment or a polar latitude. Most of the code is in the outfall and inflow routines (`node.c`, `routing.c`), routing start-up and time stepping, the climate module, and the statistics and output writers. Only BND-01 is a 5.3.0 regression (fork commit 4606020d); the other 18 are in 5.2.4 as well. 6.0.0 reproduces all 19, BND-09 and BND-12 on purpose for parity; in BND-03, BND-10, BND-17 and BND-20 its symptoms differ in part from legacy.

| ID | Issue | What happens | 5.2.4 | 6.0.0 | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [BND-01](BND-01-free-outfall-below-regulator-gets-crest-depth/) | A FREE or NORMAL outfall below a regulator gets the crest height as its depth | The outfall gets a fake tailwater: in the test a free outfall sits 3 ft above the weir crest, 346 cfs flows back at the start, and the upstream storage ends 2.58 ft too high. | no (5.3.0 regression) | reproduces | Set `z = 0` for a non-conduit link in `link_setOutfallDepth()` |
| [BND-02](BND-02-tidal-outfall-elapsed-hours/) | A TIDAL outfall reads its curve at elapsed hours, not the hour of the day | The tide is shifted by the start time of day: a run starting at 06:00 gets 0 ft at 06:00 and the 4 ft high tide at 12:00, the reverse of the curve. | yes | reproduces | Add the time-of-day part of `StartDateTime` to the elapsed time |
| [BND-03](BND-03-outfall-backflow-concentration-ignored/) | A CONCEN inflow at an outfall never sets the quality of its reverse flow | Reverse flow enters with the outfall's last discharged concentration (0 mg/L in the test instead of 100); with a FLOW inflow too, 5.2.4 and 5.3.0 put 137,300 mg/L into the network. | yes | reproduces | Make the outfall hold the CONCEN value as its quality (needs BND-04) |
| [BND-04](BND-04-outfall-backflow-mass-unbooked/) | Pollutant mass carried in by an outfall's reverse flow is not booked | Only the water entering from the outfall is booked: in the test 34 lb of pollutant enter unbooked when 4.5 lb were loaded, and the quality continuity error is -762 %. | yes | reproduces | Book the mass with the water in `removeOutflows()` |
| [BND-05](BND-05-initial-depth-uses-upstream-offset-twice/) | A conduit's inlet offset is added to the initial depth of its downstream node | A dynamic wave junction below a conduit with initial flow and an inlet offset starts too deep: 2.59 ft instead of 0.59 ft in the test, giving -19.5% routing continuity error instead of 1.1%. | yes | reproduces | Use `offset2` for the downstream node in `initNodeDepths()` |
| [BND-07](BND-07-culvert-inlet-control-adverse-slope/) | A culvert on an adverse slope loses inlet control and gets it at its outlet | Under dynamic wave, raising the outlet invert 0.04 ft drops the headwater of a 60 cfs culvert from 4.88 ft to 2.37 ft; reverse flow is inlet controlled at the outlet instead. | yes | reproduces | Apply inlet control at a reversed culvert's inlet end, to negative flow |
| [BND-08](BND-08-ponding-ignores-surcharge-depth/) | With ponding allowed, a junction ponds at its rim and ignores its surcharge depth | Turning ALLOW_PONDING on keeps a surcharged junction's head 3-10 ft below the 15 ft it should reach; the outlet pipe carries 6.2-8.7 cfs instead of 9.8-9.9 cfs. | yes | reproduces | Pond above `fullDepth + surDepth` in `setNodeDepth()` and `node_getPondedArea()` |
| [BND-09](BND-09-duration-floor-drops-last-period/) | For many end times the run stops 1 s early and loses its last period | A 00:00 to 01:03 run with 1-minute reporting writes 62 periods, not 63; 59 of 120 whole-minute run lengths are hit in 5.2.4, 10 in 5.3.0 and 6.0.0. | yes | reproduces (on purpose, for parity) | Add 0.5 s before the `floor()` of `TotalDuration` |
| [BND-10](BND-10-events-skip-rule-step-error-107/) | Control rules stop between [EVENTS]; a RULE_STEP not a multiple of ROUTING_STEP gives ERROR 107 | 5.2.4 and 5.3.0 stop evaluating rules outside events, then abort at the next event with ERROR 107 and no message in the .rpt; 6.0.0 moves a 01:00 action to 03:00. | yes | reproduces | Clamp the fixed step to the rule grid; 6.0.0: evaluate rules first |
| [BND-11](BND-11-hotstart-groundwater-flow-lost/) | A hot start drops the groundwater outflow for the first runoff step | After a hot start the groundwater inflow restarts from 0: J1 gets 1.81 cfs instead of 7.80 cfs 15 min after the restart, and 0.316 instead of 0.644 ac-ft in the first hour (-51 %). | yes | reproduces | Restore the saved flow as both `oldFlow` and `newFlow` |
| [BND-12](BND-12-timeopen-from-midnight/) | TIMEOPEN and TIMECLOSED count from midnight of the start date, not the run start | In a run starting at 06:00, `IF PUMP P1 TIMEOPEN >= 4` switches P1 off at the first step instead of 10:00; the API's TIMEOPEN reads 6.008 h after 30 s. | yes | reproduces (on purpose, for parity) | Seed `timeLastSet` with `StartDateTime`, not `StartDate` |
| [BND-13](BND-13-custom-inlet-rating-ignores-max-flow/) | Custom inlets with a RATING curve ignore the Qmax capture limit | An inlet limited to Qmax = 0.5 cfs captures 2.15 cfs of a 3 cfs street flow, 4.3 times the limit, and the sewer receives it. | yes | reproduces | Cap the rating capture at `numInlets * qMax` |
| [BND-14](BND-14-runon-to-zero-area-subcatchment/) | Runoff sent to a subcatchment with zero area disappears | All runoff from a subcatchment draining onto an Area 0 subcatchment is lost: in the test, 0.9 in of rain on 1 acre gives no runoff anywhere and a +95.3 % runoff continuity error. | yes | reproduces | Reject the input with a new ERROR 106 in `subcatch_validate()` |
| [BND-15](BND-15-negative-evaporation-adjustment/) | A negative evaporation adjustment larger than the base rate makes evaporation add water | Subcatchment surfaces gain water: in the test the runoff balance books -0.100 in of evaporation, depression storage ends above its 0.2 in capacity, and runoff is 0.396 in instead of 0.301 in. | yes | reproduces | Stop the adjusted rate at zero in `setEvap()` |
| [BND-16](BND-16-evaporation-series-start-between-entries/) | An evaporation time series uses the next entry's rate when the run starts between entries | A run starting Jan 15 on a monthly series uses 0.20 in/day instead of 0.10 in/day for 17 days; evaporation loss is 5.198 in instead of 3.500 in. | yes | reproduces | Start on the last entry at or before the start date |
| [BND-17](BND-17-polar-temperature-interpolation/) | Polar-winter temperatures from daily Tmax/Tmin jump at sunrise and never reach Tmax | At 70 N on Dec 20-21 (Tmax 40 F, Tmin 20 F), 5.2.4 and 5.3.0 peak at 36.58 F and jump 16.39 F in 10 minutes; 6.0.0 steps 19.78 F at noon. | yes | reproduces | Keep the hour of Tmax at least 1 h after sunrise |
| [BND-18](BND-18-averages-not-reset-at-report-start/) | With AVERAGES YES and a late REPORT_START, the first saved period averages from START | The first saved .out value of each node and link is the mean since START: C1 flow 0.604 cfs instead of 0.050 cfs; 5.3.0's Reported Max Depth inherits it. | yes | reproduces | Call `output_initAvgResults()` when a period before `ReportStart` is skipped |
| [BND-19](BND-19-report-start-mixes-whole-run-volumes/) | With a late REPORT_START, summary rows mix whole-run volumes with reporting-period statistics | Total Inflow Volume and outfall Total Volume count from START: J1's total inflow is 3.600 against a lateral 0.905 (10^6 ltr), and the outfall load implies 2.60 mg/L for 10 mg/L water. | yes | reproduces | Store `NodeInflow` at REPORT_START and subtract it in those columns |
| [BND-20](BND-20-summary-nan-without-routing-step/) | Summary tables print -nan when no routing step falls in the reporting period | With [EVENTS] outside the reporting period, or a toolkit run ended before its first step, the report has 27 to 29 `-nan` values (18 in 6.0.0); tools that parse the .rpt fail. | yes | reproduces | Divide by `MAX(ReportStepCount, 1)` and at least one second |
