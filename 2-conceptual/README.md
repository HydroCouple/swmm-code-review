# Conceptual and formulation issues

[All issues](../README.md)

In these issues the formulation, not the arithmetic, is at fault: two parts of the engine describe the same water differently, a volume or a pollutant mass is booked twice or not at all, or the code does not do what the reference manual says. They are in storage and conduit geometry under dynamic and kinematic wave routing (CON-01, CON-02, CON-15, CON-17, CON-18), LID units (CON-03 to CON-05, CON-14, CON-19, CON-20), snow and runoff (CON-06, CON-07, CON-16, CON-24), pollutant mass balances and treatment (CON-08 to CON-12), the summary statistics (CON-21) and control rules (CON-22, CON-23). None of them warns the user, and 14 of them leave a continuity error in the report. CON-03 and CON-04 are 5.3.0 regressions from fork commit 9d3bb8d1; the other 21 are in 5.2.4 too. 6.0.0 reproduces all 23, five of them on purpose for parity; CON-11, CON-12 and CON-21 only in part, and CON-04 and CON-05 only once IO-24 is fixed.

| ID | Issue | What happens | 5.2.4 | 6.0.0 | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [CON-01](CON-01-surcharged-storage-volume-pinned/) | The surcharge band of a closed storage unit holds water its volume never books | Depth rises through the band over the curve area while the booked volume stays full: flooding comes later and smaller, continuity error +14% and +7%, or -36% when it drains; 5.2.4 freezes the depth (+72%). | yes | reproduces | Zero a closed storage unit's own surface area above full depth |
| [CON-02](CON-02-min-surface-area-overrides-storage-curve/) | MIN_SURFAREA overrides the area curve of a small storage unit | A 4 ft² wet well fills as if it were 12.566 ft²: it ends at 2.41 ft instead of 7.56 ft, holding 9.63 of 30.25 ft³; continuity error +68.2%. | yes | reproduces | Cap a storage unit's `MIN_SURFAREA` floor at its own full-depth area |
| [CON-03](CON-03-lid-runon-to-every-partial-unit/) | Run-on onto a subcatchment that is only partly LID is counted twice | Every LID unit also gets the run-on already spread over the non-LID area: S2's runoff rises from 3.78 to 5.78 in, its peak from 20.00 to 30.21 cfs (continuity error -49.363%). | no (5.3.0 regression) | reproduces (on purpose, for parity) | Restore the full-coverage test `area == lidArea` in `lid_getRunoff()` |
| [CON-04](CON-04-covered-barrel-returns-rain-over-all-lids/) | A covered rain barrel returns the rain on every LID unit in its subcatchment | That rain is counted twice: with 20 covered barrels and a 2.3 ac rain garden on 10 ac, runoff is 1.968 instead of 1.585 ac-ft, runoff continuity error -22.7%. | no (5.3.0 regression) | reproduces (once IO-24 is fixed) | Use the barrel's own `lidArea`, not `subcatch->lidArea` |
| [CON-05](CON-05-covered-barrel-rain-lost/) | The rain falling on a covered rain barrel disappears from the water balance | The runoff continuity error equals the barrels' share of the area: +11.4% with 100 barrels covering 11.5% of 1 ac. 5.3.0 loses it only without pervious non-LID area. | yes | reproduces (once IO-24 is fixed) | Add the cover's rain to `qRunoff` when there is no pervious area |
| [CON-06](CON-06-snow-plowing-ignores-areas/) | Snow plowed to another subcatchment changes volume by the ratio of their areas | Plowed snow is multiplied by A_receiving / A_donor: from 10 ac onto 100 ac the runoff continuity error is -81.8% (16.55 ac-ft of runoff from 9.17 ac-ft); onto 1 ac, +81.8%. | yes | reproduces | Multiply each area fraction by its subcatchment's non-LID area |
| [CON-07](CON-07-groundwater-et-reduced-by-impervious-evaporation/) | Evaporation from the impervious surface reduces groundwater ET | Impervious ponding uses up the aquifer's ET budget: of two subcatchments with identical pervious areas and aquifers, the one with ponding loses 0.61 in to groundwater ET instead of 0.81 in over 10 days. | yes | reproduces (on purpose, for parity) | Add only the pervious subarea's own evaporation to `Vpevap` |
| [CON-08](CON-08-mass-load-at-dry-node-discarded/) | A MASS-type pollutant load at a node with no flow is booked, then discarded | 10 mg/s dosed for 2 h into a still pond of 2,000 ft³ leaves it at 0 instead of 1.2713 mg/L; the 0.159 lb is booked as External Inflow, quality continuity error 100%. | yes | reproduces | Mix a no-flow load into stored water, or keep it as residue |
| [CON-09](CON-09-ponded-evaporation-destroys-pollutant/) | Pollutant in water ponded on a subcatchment disappears when the water evaporates | Rain of 0.1 in at 10 mg/L on 10 ac that evaporates from depression storage loses all 2.265 lb: runoff quality continuity error 100%, or 82% with a second storm (88% in 6.0.0). | yes | reproduces (on purpose, for parity) | Keep `wPonded`, the mass infiltration and runoff did not remove |
| [CON-10](CON-10-withdrawal-removes-no-pollutant/) | A lateral withdrawal removes water from a node but no pollutant | The node's concentration exceeds every source's: 200 mg/L from 100 mg/L inflow with a 5 cfs withdrawal, so outfall loads double (897 instead of 448 lb); under KINWAVE a net withdrawal creates mass (-50%). | yes | reproduces (on purpose, for parity) | Mix with all inflowing water; withdrawals remove mass at the node's concentration |
| [CON-11](CON-11-treatment-reference-uses-wrong-pollutant-type/) | A pollutant named in a treatment equation is read by the wrong equation's type | At a storage unit the pond and inflow values are mixed up: `TP C = 0.05*TSS` sets TP to 0 instead of 5 mg/L; `TP R = 0.005*TSS` with TSS-free inflow gives 7.37, not 10. | yes | reproduces in part (on purpose, for parity) | Pick pond or inflow concentration by the evaluated equation's type |
| [CON-12](CON-12-cyclic-treatment-error-unreachable/) | ERROR 161 for a cyclic treatment dependency can never be reported | A cyclic `[TREATMENT]` set runs with error code 0 and no warning; every removal in the cycle is 0, so those pollutants are not treated at all. | yes | reproduces in part (self-reference only) | Set `ErrCode = ERR_CYCLIC_TREATMENT` (5.3.0); keep self-references in the cycle check (6.0.0) |
| [CON-14](CON-14-lid-drain-creates-washoff-mass/) | Water released by a LID underdrain is counted as removed and as delivered | BMP Removal is overstated: 46.58 lbs generated, 39.12 lbs removed and 26.93 lbs delivered (continuity -41.8%), or -8.1% in all three engines at half capture; delivered loads are right. | yes | reproduces | Book the signed `vOut1 - vOut2`, so a release reduces BMP removal |
| [CON-15](CON-15-kw-empty-conduit-inlet-area-jump/) | KINWAVE books water that never entered when inflow starts abruptly in an empty conduit | A 15 cfs step into an empty 2,000 ft pipe books 2,593 ft³ after one step, when at most 75 ft³ entered; continuity error -0.95% at 60 s and -1.24% at 5 s. | yes | reproduces | Raise a clamped rising inlet area only by what the inflow fills |
| [CON-16](CON-16-subarea-runoff-end-of-step-rate/) | Subarea runoff is booked as end-of-step rate times the step, not the water that left | A 1 ac impervious area with no losses turns 2.000 in of rain into 2.011 in of runoff at a 5-min wet step (-0.59%), 2.062 in at 15 min (-3.1%). | yes | reproduces | Book the depth the routing equation removed; volumes exact, peaks 2-6% lower |
| [CON-17](CON-17-custom-ellipse-ignores-major-axis/) | A custom elliptical pipe gets the area of a standard ellipse, whatever its span | A 2 ft x 6 ft pipe gets the area of a standard ellipse, 5.08 ft² instead of about 9.4 ft²; continuity error +3.10%. The 5.3.0 change log says this was fixed. | yes | reproduces | Compute full area and radius from rise and span (EPA's 0c0a5ca3) |
| [CON-18](CON-18-slot-area-not-monotonic/) | The Preissmann slot area shrinks as head rises and does not match the slot width | Booked conduit volume falls 151 ft³ while the head rises from 1.29 to 1.78 diameters; 458 of 756 ft³ inflow never appears in storage, continuity error 5.77% with nothing leaving. Heads are right. | yes | reproduces | Integrate the slot width to get the slot area |
| [CON-19](CON-19-swale-vegetation-fraction-inconsistent/) | Vegetative swales and permeable pavement lose track of surface water when VegFrac > 0 | The depth rate ignores 1 - VegFrac but storage uses it: with VegFrac 0.5 a swale given 1.930 in holds 0.958 in (continuity 48.6%), a pavement given 2.000 in reports 1.371 in (31.5%). | yes | reproduces | Divide `f[SURF]` by the surface void fraction, as other LIDs do |
| [CON-20](CON-20-swale-infiltration-diluted-by-imperviousness/) | A vegetative swale infiltrates at the soil's rate times the subcatchment's pervious fraction | With Horton or CN infiltration and a soil taking 1.0 in/hr, a full swale infiltrates 0.500 in/hr at 50% and 0.100 at 90% impervious; the 90% swale infiltrates 0.21 in instead of 2.13 in. | yes | reproduces | Divide pervious infiltration by the pervious area, not all non-LID area |
| [CON-21](CON-21-statistics-averaged-over-steps/) | The summary averages are means over routing steps, not over time | With a variable routing step, the Average Depth, Avg Volume, Flow Freq and Avg Flow columns over-weight the short peak-flow steps: 22% to 43% high in the test; Example7-Final J6 0.24 ft instead of 0.14. | yes | reproduces in part (not pump Avg Flow) | Accumulate value × step length and divide by time |
| [CON-22](CON-22-pump-status-on-needs-setting-one/) | A pump's `STATUS = ON` premise is false at any speed other than 1 | At half speed a pump is neither ON nor OFF for rule premises, so interlock rules take their ELSE branch: in the test an orifice closes while the pump still delivers 1.3 cfs. | yes | reproduces | Evaluate STATUS as 1 when the setting is above 0, else 0 |
| [CON-23](CON-23-control-only-gage-never-updated/) | A rain gage that only a control rule reads is never updated | A premise such as `GAGE G2 INTENSITY > 0.1` on a gage no subcatchment uses reads 0 (5.2.4, 5.3.0) or the first record (6.0.0) for the whole run, so the rule never acts. | yes | reproduces | Flag gages named in rules as used; update them without runoff |
| [CON-24](CON-24-snow-cold-content-cap/) | The snow pack's cold content is capped 12 times below the manual's limit | A 4 in pack after 72 h at 2 °F holds 0.070 in of cold content instead of 0.840 in, then melts 1.010 in instead of 0.240 in in 6 h at 50 °F. | yes | reproduces | Drop the `/ 12.0` in `updateColdContent()` |
